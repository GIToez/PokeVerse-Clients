Evolution items

Carry the item and choose Evolve on your Pokémon once it reaches the level; the item is used up. If an evolution by item and one by level both fit, the item one wins (Kirlia carrying a Dawn Stone becomes Gallade). Each item drops from the Pokémon it evolves, and from some legendaries, and the stone buyers in the cities take the ones you don't need. Shiny Pokémon evolve the same way, into the shiny form.
